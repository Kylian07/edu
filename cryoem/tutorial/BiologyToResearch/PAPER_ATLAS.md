# 📚 Paper atlas — every cryo-ET / cryo-EM paper of the Xu lab, mapped to the course

This atlas lists the cryo-electron tomography (cryo-ET) and cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM) publications of the Xu lab
that we found on the lab CV, the lab website, the AITom publication list and the lab's BibTeX file (compiled October 2026),
and shows where each one is taught in **BiologyToResearchV3**. Use it three ways:

1. **Before a module** — see which papers the module prepares you to read.
2. **After the course** — pick any paper, open its main module, then read the paper with the audit worksheet of
   [Module 26](notebooks/26_capstone_visual_proteomics.ipynb) (Section 12).
3. **As a checklist** — the course is complete when you can explain every ✅ paper's question, data, core equation,
   evidence and limitations.

**Status legend.** ✅ verified from the full text (and from the released code where it exists) ·
🟡 partially verified (abstract, supplement, code or metadata only — some details in the modules come from those sources) ·
⚠️ **content not verified — read the paper**: the module cites it but invents no details.
Current count: 81 papers — 52 ✅, 20 🟡, 9 ⚠️.

**Modules:** bold = the paper's main module (a "📄 Paper connection" box with question, method, toy, results and code);
plain = also discussed. Citations follow the published version of record; corrections to commonly circulated versions are
listed at the end.

## Corrections to commonly circulated citations

- **Pei *et al.* 2016** — the published title is "Simulating cryo electron tomograms of *crowded cell cytoplasm* for
  assessment of automated particle picking" (AITom's list paraphrases it).
- **Xu, Zhang & Alber 2009** — the title ends "…molecular density maps".
- **DISCA (PNAS 2023)** — the PNAS title is "…by *unsupervised deep iterative subtomogram clustering*"; "DISCA: … by deep
  unsupervised clustering" is the bioRxiv preprint.
- **MPP (Structure 2019)** — the arXiv preprint (arXiv:1512.09347) has a different title, "De novo visual proteomics in
  single cells through pattern mining".
- **AITom (J Struct Biol 2025)** — authors are Zhan X, **Zeng** X, Uddin MR, Xu M.
- **3D-ADA (ISMB 2019)** — the second author is **Zeng** (some BibTeX files say "Zheng").
- **COS-Net** — Front Mol Biosci 7:**613347** (not "473").
- **Few-shot domain adaptation** — Bioinformatics 37(2):185–191 (2021); the published author order differs from arXiv.
- **Cryo-Shift** — Bioinformatics 38(4):977–984 (2022).
- **SHREC'20** — published in *Computers & Graphics* 91:279–289, not in the 3DOR proceedings.
- **Lü *et al.* 2020** — a BMC Bioinformatics supplement article (21 Suppl 13:391), not an APBC proceedings paper.
- **CryoSAM** — the MICCAI version of record is titled "CryoSAM: Training-free CryoET tomogram segmentation with
  foundation models"; the arXiv title omits "CryoSAM".
- **MicroFM** — first author **Xingzu** Zhan; the paper concerns fluorescence microscopy.
- **TopoFuse** labels EMPIAR-10499 as HeLa lamellae, whereas CryoSAM and TomoPicker use it as *M. pneumoniae* — a good
  critical-reading exercise (Module 24).
- **Fungal plasma membrane (Nat Commun 2025)** — annotation used EMAN2's CNN tool; the paper does not itemise the Xu lab's
  computational contribution.
