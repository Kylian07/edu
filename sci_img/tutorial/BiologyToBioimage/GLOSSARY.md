# 📘 Glossary

Short definitions of the terms used across **BiologyToBioimage**. *First met in* links to the
earliest module whose text uses the term; *Explained in* links to the module that teaches it in
depth, usually with an equation or procedure, its plain-language reading, and code. Module numbers
refer to the [roadmap](README.md#roadmap) (00–27: the course core, including 03–10's physics and
microscope instrumentation; 28–35: the research frontier; 36: the capstone).

|---|---|---|---|
| **Active contour (snake)** | A curve that evolves to minimise an energy balancing smoothness against fit to image edges; segments by deforming a boundary rather than labelling pixels directly. | [18](notebooks/18_segmentation_classical.ipynb) | [18](notebooks/18_segmentation_classical.ipynb) |
| **Active learning** | Choosing which unlabeled images to annotate next (e.g. the ones a model is most uncertain about) rather than labeling at random, to reach good accuracy with fewer labels. | [32](notebooks/32_uncertainty_and_active_learning.ipynb) | [32](notebooks/32_uncertainty_and_active_learning.ipynb) |
