# Xu Lab Education Portal

Welcome to the Xu Lab educational repository. This portal hosts tutorial series covering scientific image analysis, deep learning in medical imaging, and computational structural biology.

## 🧭 Curriculums & Quick Links

*   **[Medical & Biological Imaging AI — Unified Tutorial Series](sci_img/tutorial/README.md)**  
    A hands-on, 4-phase (suggested 7-week) curriculum spanning PyTorch tensor foundations, classical signal/image processing, 3-D volumetric deep segmentation (MONAI), generative synthesis, and vision-language models. Contains a visual roadmap, weekly schedule, and conceptual workflow.
*   **[Cryo-EM for Computer Scientists](cryoem/readme.md)**  
    An end-to-end computational curriculum for structural biology and cryo-electron microscopy.
*   **[Machine Learning for Scientists](ml/readme.md)**  
    Foundational statistical learning theory, parameter estimation frameworks (MLE/MAP), high-dimensional regularized regression (Ridge, Lasso), generative/discriminative classification, tree ensembles (Random Forests), and distribution-free conformal uncertainty quantification.
*   **[From Biology to Machine Learning — from CMU 02-620 to the research frontier](ml/tutorial/BiologyToML/README.md)**  
    A 45-module notebook course for biology majors (no programming, calculus or statistics assumed). Level 1 teaches the full content of every 02-620 lecture — regression, classification, clustering, mixtures/EM, PCA, Bayesian networks, Gaussian graphical models, HMMs, Bayesian learning/MCMC, neural networks and model selection; Level 2 carries it to current research — single-cell and spatial omics, statistical genetics, regulatory genomics, protein language models and structure, generative design, molecular ML, causal inference, trustworthy ML and AI agents; a capstone ties it together. All data are synthetic and generated by code.
