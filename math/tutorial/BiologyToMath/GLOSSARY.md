# 📘 Glossary

Short definitions of the terms used across **BiologyToMath**. *First met in* links to the earliest module whose text
uses the term; *Explained in* links to the module that teaches it in depth, usually with a definition, an equation and its
plain-language reading, a worked example and code. Module numbers refer to the [roadmap](README.md#roadmap) (00–01:
getting started; 02–27: the 02-680 topics; 28: the capstone). Symbols are read aloud in the notation crash course of
[Module 00](notebooks/00_welcome_math_for_biology.ipynb).
